---
id: blackout-1994
title: The blackout, and the mole
year: 1994
people: [Elena, me, my mother]
---
The whole block lost power for eleven hours in July. Everyone sat on the stoops fanning themselves, and she went inside and cooked mole negro on the gas burner by candlelight. It took her three hours. She burned the chiles until the kitchen was unbreathable, opened every window, and waved a dish towel at the smoke alarm with one hand while stirring with the other. Mrs. Kowalski from upstairs came down to complain and left with a plate. When I asked why she would cook something that took three hours in the dark, she said, "La oscuridad no tiene hambre" — darkness isn't hungry. We ate on the back steps at midnight. I remember the candle wax dripping onto the foil because we had run out of holders.
