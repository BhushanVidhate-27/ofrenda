---
id: ironing-1989
title: Ironing with Pedro Infante
year: 1989
people: [Elena, me]
---
Saturday mornings she ironed everything, including things that were already flat. The radio was always on the bolero station, and when "Amorcito corazón" came on she used the iron as a microphone and sang into it, badly, with her eyes closed. Once she forgot she was holding a hot iron and pressed it into my father's good shirt. There was a brown triangle on the chest for years. She blamed the radio. "Pedro Infante distracts me," she said, as if that settled it. Nobody was allowed to throw the shirt away. It stayed in the bottom drawer with the buttons and the tin of seeds, and every so often she would take it out and laugh at the triangle all over again.
