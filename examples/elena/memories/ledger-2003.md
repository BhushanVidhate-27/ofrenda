title: The ledger she never collected
year: 2003
people: [Elena]
---
She kept a small spiral notebook in the drawer with the good scissors. In it she wrote who owed her what: twenty dollars for the electric bill, a bag of rice, two rides to the clinic, a hundred and forty for the dentist. She wrote in pencil so she could erase. Every few months my mother would say, "Mamá, they still owe you," and she would wave her hand and say she was not running a bank. Once I saw her write PAGADO next to a debt from 1999 that nobody had paid. When I asked, she said the woman had brought her tamales for a funeral and that was payment enough. I have the notebook now. The last page is blank except for a grocery list: chiles, chocolate, marigolds.
