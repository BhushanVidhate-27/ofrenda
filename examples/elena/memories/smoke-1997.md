title: The smoke was the point
year: 1997
people: [Elena, me]
---
Her mole had no recipe. She toasted the chiles until they were almost black and the whole house burned our eyes, and when we complained she said, "El humo es el punto" — the smoke is the point. She never measured anything. She measured with the palm of her hand, or with a spoon she had owned since Oaxaca, and if you asked how much, she would say "lo que pida" — whatever it asks for. Once I tried to follow her and write it down. She stopped me. "If you write it, it becomes a rule," she said. "Then it stops being mine." I have made her mole eleven times since she died. It has never been right. The last time it was too bitter, and I ate it anyway, standing at the stove, and it was closer than the others.
