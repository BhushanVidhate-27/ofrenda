title: Four in the morning
year: 1991
people: [Elena]
---
She woke at four every morning and made coffee she did not drink right away. She sat in the dark at the kitchen table with the cup in both hands and looked at nothing, and if you came in she would slide the cup toward you before you asked. She said it was the only hour that belonged to her. When I was small I thought she was sad. When I got older I thought she was praying. Now I think she was doing neither, or both, and it was not my business. That is the thing I have started to do since she died: sit at the kitchen table before the sun with a cup going cold. I do not know if I inherited an altar or a habit. My mother says both, and puts the marigolds out on November first.
