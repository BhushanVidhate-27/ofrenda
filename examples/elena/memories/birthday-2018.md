title: The last birthday phone call
year: 2018
people: [Elena, me]
---
She turned eighty in 2018 and told everyone she did not want a party, which was a lie, but she was too tired to plan one. I called from college at ten at night. She answered on the third ring and immediately asked if I had eaten. When I asked what she was doing, she said nothing, just sitting. Then she sang "Las Mañanitas" to herself over the phone, very quietly, because she said if nobody else was going to sing it she would do it herself. She stopped in the middle to complain that the phone made her voice sound like a stranger. I asked her to keep going. She did. I have the voicemail somewhere. I have never been able to listen to it twice in a row.
