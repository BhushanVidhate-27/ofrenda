title: La pena se va caminando
year: 
people: [Elena, me]

When I was nine, my dog got out and was hit on Ashland, and I would not stop crying. She did not tell me it was in heaven. She put on her shoes and her coat over her nightgown, took my hand, and walked me around the block. It was cold. She was wearing slippers because she could not find her shoes by the door. We did not talk for the first block. Then she said, "La pena se va caminando" — sorrow leaves by walking. We walked eleven blocks, past the church and the laundromat and the man selling elotes even though it was winter, and by the time we got home I had stopped. I do not think she ever told me what she did with her own sorrow. I only ever saw her walk.
