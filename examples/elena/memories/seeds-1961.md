id: seeds-1961
title: A sewing machine and a tin of seeds
year: 1961
people: [Elena, Abuelo Tomás]
---
She came north from Oaxaca with two suitcases and a sewing machine, and inside the machine's drawer, a tin of marigold seeds wrapped in a sock. The apartment on 18th Street had one window that got sun, so that is where the tin went. The first winter killed half of them. She planted the rest anyway, in coffee cans, on the sill, and told my mother they were "para la vista" — for the view. By March there were four orange heads pressed to the glass like they were waiting for someone. She never called it a garden. She called it "las flores," and if you touched them without asking she would smack your hand with a wooden spoon.
